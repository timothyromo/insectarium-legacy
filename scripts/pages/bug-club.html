Bug Club

We get a lot of requests to volunteer at the insectarium, but due to labor laws, we cannot. Only nonprofits are allowed to have volunteers. If you would like to be involved at the insectarium, learn skills like animal husbandry, identification, museum curation, terrarium design, and more - join our bug club! 

Perks
  • Free passes to the museum for yourself and guests
  • Free lessons to learn how to pin insects, create animal habitats, handle live bugs, and more!
  • Free or discounted tickets to events held by the Insectarium
  • Special “behind the scenes” access to some of the museum’s animals
  • Discount at the gift shop
  • Discounted live bugs (when available)
  • Free feeder bugs (when available) 
  • Club days and events​
Available activities, training, and educational opportunities​
  • Learn basics of entomology, ecology, and evolution
  • Animal husbandry, breeding programs
  • Museum curation
  • Curriculum design
  • Science communication and outreach
  • Classes and workshops
  • Social media, graphic design, photography
​Requirements
  • Must be 16 years of age or older
  • Sign waiver
  • If under 18, we need a signed waiver from an adult guardian to participate
  • Visit the insectarium before applying to be in the Bug Club
  • Follow code of conduct
  • Attend a meeting to discuss interests and ways you want to be involved
  • Make an effort to participate in one activity per month or at least respond to our emails (or you may be removed from the list)
  • Dues are $5 per month or $10 for 3 months, if dues are not paid you cannot participate or utilize the perks for that month (your free passes will be inactive) but will be reinstated once dues are paid again.
  • No refunds on dues, even if you do not participate or utilize any perks
