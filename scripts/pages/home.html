Thursday Admission Changes
- Thursdays are our discounted admission day!
- Admission is $4 plus a service fee if you book online
- Walk-in admission is $6
- Booking online will guarantee there is space for you
- Walk-ins may have to wait if we are fully booked
Important notes before you visit
- Our space is small! We can fit hundreds of bugs in here but not that many people. Reservations are not required most days but are highly recommended, especially on weekends and school breaks.
- We limit capacity to keep our space accessible for people with wheelchairs and other mobility devices, and parents with strollers. Also to abide by the fire code.
- Do not book tickets and then bring extra people with you because there weren't any left. 
- You can sign up for the waitlist and you will be notified of any openings.
- Please don't come inside before your appointment time, we need to wait for the previous group to leave and also need time to clean up between groups.
- We are actively looking for a larger space. Thank you for understanding!
If you have visited us before, please fill out our accessibility survey! This will help us understand accommodations we may need to add or adjust so everyone can enjoy our space. ​
