PDX Insectarium Summer Camp

Join us for an exciting hands-on summer camp designed for young bug enthusiasts (ages 8-18). Each day focuses on different aspects of entomology, from animal care to pinning and field identification. Choose individual days or sign up for the full experience at a discount!
