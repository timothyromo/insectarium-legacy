Birthday Party or Field Trip
Do you have a bug-loving kiddo with a birthday coming up? Or a small class that would like to visit for a field trip? Our space is small, but we can accommodate up to 15 people total (20 in summer when people can be out front). Must be at least one adult chaperone if kids are under 16 years old (two if kids will be outside).

Booking a private event allows for more people (general admission is capped at 10 people), all of our live animal handling options are included (some cost extra or are not available during general admission), you get the space for your group only, and extra staff are here to answer your questions and facilitate handling. Tarantula handling is only available if you let us know ahead of time and only if the group is calm and quiet the entire visit. Loud noises stress tarantulas and nobody wants to hold a stressed tarantula!

Rates are $200 for first hour, $100 for each subsequent hour. $50 deposit to hold the date. The remainder will be due on the day of the event. We can provide tables, seating, table cloths, activities, and bugs. Food and drinks to be provided by you. For birthday parties we have a gift for the birthday person and for field trips we have printed take-home materials available by request.

Private Pinning Class
Learn the techniques of relaxing, pinning, and drying dead insect specimens. Includes instruction, pins, forceps, styrofoam pinning block, a shadowbox, and 1-2 dead insects (depending on size/type). The type of insect will depend on what is available at the time of booking. When you request to schedule, I will email you the available options. Please respond to this email or I will not be able to accept the appointment request.

Recommended age 10+ as pinning requires fine motor skills and uses sharp objects. Children under age 15 must have a parent/guardian attending with them. If the parent does not wish to pin an insect, there will be a $10 admission fee when you arrive for the class.

​This booking software does not allow for multiple tickets/purchases, so the $75 is a deposit if more than one person is attending. If additional people are attending, payment is due at the insectarium the day of the event: $75 if pinning an insect and $12 if not. Limit 10 people, whether or not they are pinning an insect. The class is scheduled for one hour, but pinning will take 20-40 min, depending on the insect and skill level, so the rest of the time you can just explore the zoo! If you want more time to explore the zoo, depending on the time you book for the pinning lesson, you can book the general admission time slot before or after pinning or add a private tour ($25 per person) if we are not open for general admission at that time.
