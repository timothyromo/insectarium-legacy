Live Bug Photo Shoots

Get some dreamy portraits taken with live bugs! Our live bug options are a tarantula, mantis, millipede, stick/leaf insects, cockroaches, and scorpion (sometimes). As live animals are unpredictable, we can't guarantee all of these will be available at all times. We can accommodate children and groups of up to three.
​
$50 deposit to hold the date and anything remaining will be due the day of the shoot. One person may attend who is not participating in the shoot (ie parent chaperone), any additional guests that have not been paid for as models will be charged a $12 admission fee. Limit three models, limit six people total. When you request to book, we will send you a form to fill out. You must complete the form before we will accept your request and book the appointment.
