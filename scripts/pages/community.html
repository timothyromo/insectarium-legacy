Seeking Community Partners

We’re looking for more community partners and would love to collaborate!
Some ways to get involved:
  • Joint Events – Want to bring bugs to your space but can’t afford our rates? Let’s team up for a joint event and share the profits!
  • Discounted Visits – If you run a school, daycare, foster care group, sober group, nature club, book club, or similar organization, we can offer special discount codes for regular visits.
  • Host an Event – Have a skill or workshop to share but need a space? Rent our insectarium at a set fee and sell your own tickets, or we can co-host and split the profits.
  • Sell Your Goods – While we can’t manage consignment, we’d love to buy from local makers at a discount to resell in our shop (no minimum).
  • Business & Community Connections – We’re creating a business card/brochure exchange and want to offer reciprocal discounts and rewards with local businesses.
​Email us at info@pdxinsectarium.org with your ideas!
