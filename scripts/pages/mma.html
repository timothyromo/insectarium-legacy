MMA: Mini Museum Alliance

What is a Mini Museum? A small, independently-owned museum or exhibition of a themed or curated collection that is available for public viewing.

What is the Mini Museum Alliance? Brought to you by four Mini Museums: The Freakybuttrue Pecuiliarium, PDX Insectarium, The Zymoglyphic Museum, and StarTropics Pinball Museum, the MMA aims to bring together all local museums of small stature. Network, collaborate, advise, and join forces! 

More info coming soon! Have questions or want to join? Email us at minimuseumpdx@gmail.com. Follow us on instagram @minimuseumpdx

Some questions you may have:

I have a mini museum or collection I would like to exhibit, can I join?

Yes! We are actively looking for new people who are passionate about museums, collect or curate something interesting, and want to share that with the public.  

What do I get if I join the MMA?

If you're just getting started, or don't know where to start, we are here to advise and provide support. If you have an established museum or other related experience, we have cross-promotional and collaborative opportunities. Access to a community of professionals, collectors, and creatives. Contribute to the creation and growth of the Mini Museum Alliance!

- Communicate your knowledge and ideas with the public
